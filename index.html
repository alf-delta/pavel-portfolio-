Approach

How I work

Four ideas that show up in every project, whatever the industry.

01

Fix the route, not the engine

Traffic poured into a broken process is vanity. Before touching the media budget I map where data breaks and where leads leak.

02

Economics before scale

Growth without profit is vanity. I work from cohorts and LTV/CAC, and cut channels that bring volume but burn cash.

03

One source of truth

CRM, analytics, product data and ad platforms feed a single picture of the business. If it isn’t tracked, it didn’t happen.

04

Owners, not hands

I build small teams of specialists with clear ownership, rhythm and KPIs, so work moves without constant oversight.

Forensic GTM

Most strategies copy the category leader. I start from the data and look for behavioral vacuums: gaps in demand that competitors are structurally unable to see or fill.

I’ve done this as head of e-commerce, agency practice lead and growth strategist across FMCG, beauty, MedTech and tourism — from Publicis Groupe and ALIDI to BeWell Medical and NYC Water Cruises.

Input (market)
Crisis, panic, falling demand.
Analysis
A structural shift in price tiering — for example the “masstige vacuum”.
Output
A growth engine built on measured demand, not creative guessing.

Services

What I can take on

Four kinds of work, each aimed at one broken system.

Zero-to-one system build

I package the product, find the first groups that respond, test the language the audience uses for its problem, and run the route from first signs of demand to the first paying customers.

Good fit whenA product exists, but revenue is unstable.

Revenue & unit-economics audit

Cohort and LTV/CAC analysis to see real profitability: which channels earn and which only burn cash. Then I rebuild the monetary cycle, not just the ads.

Good fit whenThe business is scaling on broken logic.

Data route & MarTech architecture

CRM, analytics, ad platforms and product data (PIM) in one source of truth, so customer signals turn into memory instead of noise.

Good fit whenSignals are collected but never structured.

Crisis re-alignment & team design

When the graphs drop: freeze non-essential spend, audit the funnel for leaks, re-point the message — and assemble the specialist team that can run it.

Good fit whenRevenue has stalled, or the vision has no operating system.
